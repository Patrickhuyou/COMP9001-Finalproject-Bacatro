Project Title: Bacatro(temporarily)
Concept:
A Pygame-based roguelike game inspired by Balatro, where the core gameplay revolves around Baccarat (the card game). Players progress through six increasingly difficult levels by winning Baccarat rounds to accumulate chips. Each level has a target chip amount and a shrinking bet limit that must be managed to advance. Between rounds, players collect common, rare, and epic evolution cards that modify game mechanics (e.g. altering payout odds, strengthening pair bets, revealing hints, adding extra bets, forcing extra draws).

Advanced Concepts I Plan to Demonstrate:
- Classes & objects split across modules (`Card`, `Hand`, `Shoe`, `GameState`, and item classes)
- Inheritance through the item system
- Flow control through Baccarat third-card rules and round outcome branches
- File I/O through JSON save/load and leaderboard files
- Testing through unit tests for cards, rules, payouts, and storage
- Recursion through hand-total calculation and leaderboard insertion
- Exception handling for save/load failures
- Decorators or higher-order functions for item effects modifying game rules

Target Audience: Casual gamers who enjoy card games and strategic decision-making.
